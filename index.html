Overseas-qualified architect and result-driven BIM / digital construction specialist.

Covering the whole project lifecycle, from early design concepts and 2D/3D modelling to construction drawings, site work and the operational stage.

Saad graduated from Al-Nahrain University with a BSc in Architectural Engineering, first-class honours (A+). His graduation project was named the best among all Iraqi students in the world in 2019. He went on to earn an MSc in Construction Management with BIM, with distinction, from Coventry University.

He is skilled across architecture, construction and BIM: building information modelling, construction management and coordination, plus the related BIM management, design, visualisation and construction software.
