About

A payments specialist since his teens, now focused on the technology shaping the economy, from paytech and fintech to the companies building what comes next.

Ciaran’s ties to the card payments industry go back to an early age. He started out in customer service, telesales, B2B appointment setting and field sales with some of the biggest names in UK payments, including Elavon, Paymentsense, Cardsave and Payzone.

In 2017 he founded Card Industry Professionals (CIP), an ISO with direct partnerships with acquirers, gateway providers, terminal makers and finance companies. Shift4 acquired CIP in 2024, and Ciaran went on to lead Growth & Cross Sell for the UK and EU. He now invests through Founders Capital.

The journey

From checking card applications at 16 to selling his company to a NYSE-listed payments business.

2007

First job in payments: Elavon

After his GCSEs, he spent the summer checking merchant applications and handling customer calls at EuroConnex, which became Elavon while he was there.

2010

Youngest field representative: Paymentsense

At 18 he was the youngest field rep in the business. He ran his own diary and signed merchants across Yorkshire and Lincolnshire, often installing their terminals himself.

2012 — 2017

Cardsave and Payzone UK

He set up appointments for field sales teams, worked on pricing and card-acceptance enquiries, and converted deals by phone, including change-of-tenancy merchants.

2017

Founded Card Industry Professionals

He started CIP to give small businesses transparent card-payment pricing, without unnecessary charges, and long-term relationships.

2022

Managing Director, CIP

He oversaw strategic partnerships, business processes, the product roadmap and relationships with investors and stakeholders.

2024

Acquired by Shift4 (NYSE: FOUR)

CIP joined Shift4, and Ciaran became Vice President, SMB, bringing Shift4 payments and SkyTab POS to small and medium-sized businesses.

2025

VP Growth & Cross Sell Europe, and Founders Capital

He led growth strategy for Shift4’s EU card-present business, and became a private investor with Founders Capital, backing companies from Seed to Pre-IPO.
