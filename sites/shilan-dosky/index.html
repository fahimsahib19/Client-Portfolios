I · Profile

A career in public service

Connecting the Kurdistan Region with Europe, and turning priorities into action at the highest level of government.

Shilan Dosky is Head of Special Projects and Communications at the Office of the Prime Minister of the Kurdistan Regional Government in Erbil. She has held the role since 2019.

Her career began in the Netherlands, where she chaired the Kurdistan Women Union in Europe from 2007 to 2012 and represented the KDYU at the IYDU. She later served as Advisor for European Affairs at the KRG Department of Foreign Relations, then as Head of the EU Unit at the Kurdistan Democratic Party’s Foreign Relations Office.

She studied Political Science at the Free University.
