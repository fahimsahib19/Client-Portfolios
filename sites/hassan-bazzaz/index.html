01 — About

“Aspire to inspire.” An architect by training and a project leader by practice, Hassan turns government priorities into plans, schedules and delivered results.

Hassan began his career at Khatib & Alami, where he managed budgets, planned work across multiple cities and oversaw how tasks were distributed. He then worked in Dubai as a functional consultant at Odoo, configuring business systems for clients.

Since 2022 he has worked with the Kurdistan Regional Government. He started as a Senior Analyst, moved up to Project Coordinator, and now leads projects in the Priority Unit at the Office of the Prime Minister. He holds an M.Sc. in Engineering Project Management from Coventry University, where he was ranked first in his cohort.
