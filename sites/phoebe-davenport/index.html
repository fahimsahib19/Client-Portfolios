About

Phoebe is a Senior Global Enterprise Account Executive at Revolut. Before that, she spent over five years at Worldpay as a Senior ISO/ISV Partnership Development Manager, and nearly three at American Express as a Merchant Acquisition Manager.

Her sales roots are in Spain, in luxury fashion with Pronovias and Adolfo Dominguez, in hospitality, and teaching English. She was named a Top Performer at American Express in 2019 and 2020, and won Worldpay's Circle of Excellence in 2022 and 2023.
